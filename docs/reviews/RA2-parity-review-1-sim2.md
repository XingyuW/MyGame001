## Confirmed findings

### S2-01 — P0 — IFV repair uses pre-parasite HP and can resurrect a killed vehicle or act after its own death

Primary location: `crates/sim/src/special/vehicles.rs:387` (also 262, 286–351, 361, 390, 412, 425–426).

`vehicles::advance` builds `index = actors(world)` before applying due parasite damage. The first loop calls `detonate_one`, reads the live resulting HP, and on death releases the parasite. The following IFV loop reuses the original index: both the repair source (`a.alive()`) and target (`b.alive()` and `b.hp < max`) are assessed from stale positive HP. The final write unconditionally subtracts the negative repair damage from live HP.

This is reachable with shipped Rules-present mechanics. Stock DroneJump is Damage50/reload80, Parasite is 100% against a green Grizzly, and a publicly loaded Engineer selects IFVMode1/RepairBullet, Damage−50/range461/reload107. With a 10HP attached host, a due Drone hit changes HP to −40; an owned ready Engineer-IFV within range selects the stale 10HP snapshot and changes it back to 10 before Cleanup. The public timing premise can be established by loading the nearby Engineer into the IFV in Commands of that due tick, so an earlier repair does not cure the Drone first; alternatively the ready IFV enters range during the preceding Movement stage. The kill was already recorded and the Drone was released, yet the vehicle survives. Conversely, a parasite-killed repair IFV remains a candidate source in the same loop and can repair another unit after death. The same-tick stage order is `special::advance` → infantry/vehicles advance → Combat → Cleanup (`schedule.rs`). No lower helper restores liveness validation.

Expected: validate current positive HP for both repair actors and targets at the action/write boundary; dead actors cannot perform or receive ordinary healing. Preserve living repair and parasite-removal behavior. `ra6_vehicles.rs:457` checks ordinary range/reload and `:1283` checks removing a Drone **before its first damage tick**; neither tests lethal due-tick damage followed by repair or a killed IFV source. New regressions must exercise both live-state boundaries through the schedule. This is an incorrect simulation result, not waivable as the historical LIVE-01/DRONE-01 debt.

Expected: frozen launchers cannot initiate the remaining bay launches; preserve documented pending-mission policy and already flying actors.
