## Current state

MyGame001 is a playable single-player, isometric base-building RTS for Apple Silicon macOS, implemented in Rust/Bevy. Players gather ore, build a powered base, produce units, and win by destroying every enemy building. Twelve skirmish maps (nine of them large painted environment maps), two legacy opponent armies and three difficulties are implemented.

The committed baseline is M8. A substantial integrated follow-up adds the unified 72-item arsenal, HD artwork, improved controls, mining orders, team colors, Super Me and richer terrain. The latest step is an **RA2 visual direction pass** that replaces the flat, cartoon-like art with Blender pre-rendered miniatures, seamless layered terrain and a machined-metal sidebar. All of this is still in the working tree: preserve both modified tracked files and untracked files/assets.

### RA2 visual direction pass (2026-10-05, working tree)

The user judged the HD set "too cartoonish" and asked for a Red Alert 2 look. Diagnosis: the Pillow rasterizer gave each polygon one flat brightness, palettes were saturated primaries, vehicles were boxes with full-length team stripes, and each terrain diamond had independent noise (a visible grid). Westwood pre-rendered RA2 from 3D models, so this pass does the same with the project's own original geometry. Before/after: [`docs/screenshots/ra2-pass/before-after.png`](screenshots/ra2-pass/before-after.png); full native captures are in the same directory.

**Pipeline** (all original; no RA2 media):

| Step | Tool | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/generate_hd_terrain.py` | `uv` Python + Pillow/NumPy | Grass (`terrain/0`) and water (`2`) bases, the soil overlay (`4`), particles; geometry for terrain tops |
| `scripts/render_hd_blender.py` | Blender 5.1 Cycles (Metal GPU), headless | Colour and team-mask passes per building, unit facing and terrain top, in `~/Library/Caches/Codex/reviews/MyGame001/ra2-visual/render` |
| `scripts/finish_hd_art.py` | `uv` Python + Pillow/NumPy | Shipped sprites: team-paint encoding, 8-facing sheets, top atlases (`terrain/1,3,5,6,7`) |

- `render_hd_blender.py` subclasses `generate_hd_art.Model`, so every existing `building()`/`unit()` definition renders unchanged: real orthographic 2:1 dimetric camera matched pixel-exactly to `Model.project` (x/y swapped for Blender's handedness), bevelled edges, smooth-by-angle curves, a principled material per class (painted team metal, steel, dark metal, concrete, glass, organic) with remapped, lower-saturation colours, procedural blotch/rain-streak/grain grime and AO, warm sun from the upper right and a shadow catcher (shadows fall up-left into sprite headroom). Full render is about 30 minutes.
- **Team paint stays compatible with `presentation.rs` without Rust changes.** Lit renders cannot satisfy its hue rules in shadow, so a flat mask pass decides ownership. `finish_hd_art.py` re-encodes masked pixels onto the exact authored blue ramp (`Y_REF`/`Y_GAIN` set the luminance mapping) and nudges neutral pixels that would accidentally match the blue or red rule.
- New geometry: `Model.tube`/`Model.prism` plus `begin`/`end` smoothing groups; a rewritten `ground_vehicle()` (sloped glacis, tracks or wheels with road wheels, tapered turret, mantlet, long barrel with muzzle brake, team turret/cab/hull band, true proportions instead of the old `(1.06, 1.3, .58)` squash); rounded infantry (limbs, head, helmet, rifle, team webbing); an open ore hopper; aircraft ownership plate lifted out of the fuselage and a painted Nighthawk tail fin.
- **Terrain is layered** (`battlefield.rs`): every cell gets a grass or water base, sampled from textures periodic over an 8×8 cell lattice (slot = `(x%8, y%8)`) so neighbours always match and no grid shows. Soil, ore, cliff and shoreline cells add a 2×-size soil overlay with a ragged feathered edge (4×4 lattice), giving soft transitions and beaches. Ore, cliffs, roads, groves and cottages are Blender-rendered tops with baked shadows. Overlay sprites take the least-revealed sight of their 3×3 neighbourhood so they never paint over shroud or the void. Ore tops fade out once a visible cell is mined out, replacing the old image swap. Every atlas is 1024² to satisfy the bounded PNG loader (4 MiB file, 16 MiB decode); bases use 2 texels per world pixel, which is still more than 2× oversampled at observed zoom.
- Sidebar: gunmetal panel with per-side `BorderColor` bevels on the panel, buttons and production cards; amber tab/readiness highlights; amber credits and green/red power text. Layout and input are unchanged.
- The previous art and generators are backed up in `~/Library/Caches/Codex/reviews/MyGame001/ra2-visual/` (`hd-before/`, old scripts).

### Latest RTS controls and battlefield follow-up

- Continuous anchored scrolling: hold middle mouse or Space + left mouse, displace from the press point, and keep scrolling while held. Returning to the anchor stops movement. Focus loss cancels the gesture. Arrow keys and minimap navigation remain; new settings default edge scrolling off.
- Selection uses visible unit body centers with live drag preview. Leaving the window preserves the last valid drag endpoint. Double-click selects the visible type; Shift and control groups remain available. Sidebar clicks cannot issue world commands, and accepted orders show destination feedback.
- A compact metal sidebar with smaller icons, **six cards per page**, status badges and prerequisite tooltips.
- Right-click or attack-move over ore assigns miners a remembered field. They unload at owned refineries, return, and seek reachable remaining ore on depletion. Explicit Move/Stop/Attack overrides the cycle.
- Optional human-only **Super Me**: each item costs 1 credit and advances 10 production progress units per eligible tick. Prerequisites, power penalties, placement, queue and exit rules still apply; discrete completion rounds to whole ticks. AI and ordinary matches retain normal prices/speeds. Restart preserves selections and deterministic hashing includes the bonus state.
- Six selectable ownership colors, with a distinct opponent color. Recoloring preserves neutral materials, uses original pixels on restart, and covers units, buildings, icons and minimap.
- Connected roads, forests, static cottages, rocky terraces, varied ground, water detail and ore depletion. Shared terrain layouts write real obstacles into TMX; base space, resources, symmetry and connectivity are checked.

Cliffs are blocked terraces, not traversable height/ramp systems. Cottages are static obstacles, not garrisonable buildings. Matches remain two-player, and there is no save-game serialization pipeline. Details: [RTS_IMPROVEMENTS.md](RTS_IMPROVEMENTS.md).

### Flight, rally points, defeat notices and ocean maps (2026-10-09, working tree)

- **Aircraft fly.** Units of the air domain are drawn 64 world pixels up (rocketeers 40), climb from the ground where they are built, land beside their air base while rearming, and cast a separate shadow (a darkened copy of their sprite, `AirShadow` in `crates/game/src/units.rs`) on the ground cell they are over. Their five sprite sheets were re-rendered without the baked shadow. `Altitudes` tells `fog.rs` how high to draw shots and projectiles; a selected aircraft shows a plumb line to its ground cell.
- **Factories never jam.** `sim::building::exit_cell` searches outward from a full ring over passable cells (up to 24 steps), so a completed unit leaves at the nearest free cell instead of waiting inside. It still waits when no ground around can be reached.
- **Per-building rally points.** A left click on a unit-producing building selects it (`SelectedBuilding` in `selection.rs`); a right click on the map then issues `SetRallyPoint` for that building alone, and its rally point is drawn as a flag. `R` and the Rally button still set every producer's.
- **Defeat notices.** `crates/game/src/notices.rs` lists "Red Player has been defeated" in the top left corner in the defeated player's team colour for nine seconds.
- **Three ocean maps** (`pacific_atoll`, `iron_isthmus`, `arctic_floes`; 15 skirmish maps in all, `MAX_MAPS` is 16). Their land is a tree, so the sea is one connected piece. The only land route between mirrored bases must be at least about 13 cells wide or computer players stall on it.
- Review captures: `MYGAME_FEATURE_REVIEW=<dir>` and `MYGAME_MAP_REVIEW=<dir>` with `MYGAME_MAP_REVIEW_FROM=<map id>`; results in `docs/screenshots/flight-rally-ocean/`.

### Realistic redesign of buildings and units (2026-10-10, working tree)

The user judged the RA2-style set "so ugly" and set a new standard: the art must look real. Every
one of the 31 buildings and 53 units was remodelled from real-world reference dimensions; the
terrain is unchanged. Diagnosis of the old set: hulls squashed wide for readability, whole roofs
and hull sides in saturated team blue, mixed authoring units (cells and pixels) that hid wrong
proportions, and one shared material.

- **Authoring in metres.** `scripts/hd_kit.py` is the modelling kit (boxes, extrusions, lofts,
  surfaces of revolution, trusses, masts, rails, a transform stack) with one scale per model
  (`Kit(m, u)`, cells per metre) and a palette of `C(rgb, material)` colours. `hd_arch.py` holds
  architectural parts for a 10 m cell (walls, windows, roofs, stacks, tanks, pipe runs, fences,
  dishes, cranes are in the building files). Models: `hd_buildings.py` (+ `_a`, `_b`, `_c`),
  `hd_vehicles.py`, `hd_infantry.py`, `hd_aircraft.py`, `hd_naval.py`; `hd_units.py` dispatches.
  `generate_hd_art.py` keeps the canvas, the flat preview and the terrain helpers.
- **One shader per material class** in `render_hd_blender.py`: painted steel (dust on upward
  faces, soot in recesses, dirt near the ground, paint chipped to steel on edges), concrete with

## Architecture and where to work

The simulation runs at **20 ticks/second**, using integer arithmetic and deterministic state hashing. `game` turns input into commands via `CommandSink` / `SimHost`; `sim` owns the rules. During matches, mutations go through `CommandQueue`. Pre-tick match setup is a separately documented initialization path.

The ordered stages are Commands → AI → Economy → Pathfinding → Movement → Combat → Cleanup → Visibility → WinCondition → EndOfTick. Preserve schedule ordering and ambiguity checks when extending specialist systems.
